# Fortis Colosseum: simulator policy (waves 1–11 into Sol)

How the simulator runs a Fortis Colosseum run where the [facts spec](./fortis-colosseum-facts-spec.md)
stops, and how practice starts behave. Each rule names its basis: **Captures** (facts spec),
**W** (OSRS Wiki, shorthands as in the facts spec), **Policy** (a simulator decision with no direct
evidence, or chosen between consistent readings). What would settle each policy is in
[`fortis-colosseum-open-questions.md`](./fortis-colosseum-open-questions.md). Wave 12's own rules are
the existing Sol Heredit ones ([facts](./sol-heredit-facts-spec.md),
[register](./sol-heredit-unverified.md)).

## Run, identity and replay

| Topic | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | The encounter stays `sol_heredit`, routed at `/colosseum` and displayed as *Fortis Colosseum*. The former `/sol-heredit` link opens a direct Sol start (wave 12), saved as a setup pick, and the address becomes `/colosseum/`. Setting `colosseumStartWave` takes 1–12; missing or invalid means 12. | Policy |
| Direct wave 12 | Unchanged: today's loadout, default modifier set (Blasphemy III), camera, spawn, random streams and timing. Snapshots without wave metadata are direct Sol. `state.phase` stays Sol's phase. No persistence migration or replay-version bump for the rename. | Policy (compatibility) |
| One run | One live encounter, one recording, one player. A `SolWaveDirector` owns Sol's part; Sol's NPC-band order and seeded stream are untouched. The director is given the world tick C at which Sol appears; Sol's opening deadlines shift by C, while timers already scheduled from an event's own tick do not. | Policy |
| Random streams | Wave layout and modifier offers each draw from their own seeded stream, separate from Sol's and from each other, so a choice or a layout never shifts Sol's draws. | Policy (determinism) |
| Arena | The open waves arena is baked once. At C the 48 shield-warrior locs are installed for both collision and visuals. A direct wave-12 start is enclosed from C = 0. | Captures (fence added at C: A:4976, K:3998); one bake is Policy |

## Wave flow

| Topic | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Completion | Tc is the **removal** of the last required fighter, not its lethal hit. The Quartet member counts; Bee swarms, Solarflare orbs and healing totems do not. | Captures (27/27 removal); Quartet counting is Policy (never isolated) |
| Offers | Open at Tc+3. An accepted confirmation at Tk schedules T0 = Tk+5. Minimus is not presented (deferred). | Captures (27/27, 30/30) |
| Player | Keeps their tile, HP, prayer, supplies and timers through waves 1–11; nothing is reset at a boundary. | Captures (no teleport at 30 boundaries); keeping state is Policy |
| Reinforcements | At T0+66 in every wave still active, **including a slow wave 1** (W-Strat's jaguar). A wave cleared on T0+66 wins the tie: no reinforcement spawns. | Captures (38/38 at +66); wave 1: W-Strat; tie: Policy for W's "automatically killed" |
| Wave cleanup | Bee swarms and Solarflare orbs still present are removed at completion and return at the next T0+5 and T0; any healing totem still present is removed too (a guard: none was ever captured outliving its wave). Pending Solarflare hits are dropped with the orbs. | Captures (Tc deletions); totem guard and dropped hits: Policy (C17) |
| Doom | Doom **points** reset to 0 at T0 and the tier is kept; the reset message is sent only when points were held, including at wave 12's T0. Javelin artillery adds **two** stacks, shown as one splat and one message with the new count. Blasphemy drains for artillery like any other enemy damage taken off-prayer. | Captures (C:1641, A:4970; none at C:2863, C:3445); two stacks: W-NPC Javelin Colossus rev 15411467 and W-Strat rev 15414089, UNVERIFIED in captures; presentation and the Blasphemy drain (cache struct 894 "damage received from enemies off-prayer"): Policy (C18) |
| Solarflare | Four orbs at T0 on (38,40), (27,40), (27,29), (38,29), first step T0+1, each on its captured side-4 square at the tier's step and corner timing (the Sol routes' tier timing). Removed at Tc. The orbs cannot be attacked, directly or by a ricochet. | Captures (A waves 5–11, 28/28 orbs; `CAPTURED_WAVE_SOLARFLARE`); not attackable: Policy |
| Totemic | Every wave fighter, warband and Quartet member included, is a target: one totem per fighter, added on its first tick at or below 50% HP (SW diagonal, else NE; other NPCs do not block; with no free tile it retries next tick), healing floor(30%) of its max HP on the existing totem cadence. The totem is removed on the tick its target dies. | Captures (Run A, 55 totems; threshold, tile, heal); eligibility of every fighter and same-tick removal (captured 0–1 ticks later): Policy (C20) |
| Dynamic Duo | Each shockwave gets a partner on the nearest SW tile (squared distance from the primary's SW tile) whose 3×3 footprint lies on the waves floor and overlaps neither the primary nor any other NPC spawning at T0, the warband (Quartet included) among them; ties go south, then west (lower y, then lower x). Draws no random number, so owning it moves no other roll. | W-Mod rev 15163385, W-Strat rev 15414089 ("near"/"around" the first); placement: Policy (C8) |
| Red Flag | Minotaurs spawn as 12813: the production melee route search toward cardinal reach of the player's start-of-tick tile, one tile per tick, and it does not block other NPCs. Stats, attacks, heal and regeneration are 12812's. The unverified choices (speed, other NPCs still blocking its own steps, the step out from under the player, walking to the nearest reachable tile when no route reaches) are listed in `minotaur.ts`. | W-Mod, W-NPC Minotaur (routefinding), W-Strat rev 15414089 (others move into its tiles); the rest Policy (C8) |
| Volatility | On a wave NPC's removal tick: its explosion, and its gib on its south-west tile for 3 ticks. The jaguar takes the human gib (12828) and the Red Flag minotaur 12813 the minotaur gib (12830). | Captures (Run A, 33/33); the jaguar and 12813 mappings: Policy (C16) |
| Wave 12 | From the final T0 (no `Wave: 12` message): the jump message at T0+1, the player relocated at T0+4, Sol added and the fence closed at C = T0+6. The jump message is not repeated at C+1; that substitute line is only for a direct wave-12 start. | Captures (A:4970–4976, K:3992–3998) |

## Modifier offers

| Topic | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Configured set | One modifier set serves every start wave; there is no separate direct-Sol preset and no generated pick for a skipped intermission. Until the player edits it, the set is the start wave's default: none for wave 1, Blasphemy I for wave 2, Blasphemy II for waves 3–5 and Blasphemy III from wave 6 (wave 12's is the direct-Sol default above). A set the player edits is an explicit override, which changing the start wave leaves as it is. A start on waves 1–11 owns the set going into its first wave, where it takes effect at T0 with that wave's pick; a direct wave-12 start has it in force from tick 0. | Policy (the defaults approximate what a run usually holds by then) |
| First offer | The game's run opens wave 1 with nothing owned and offers **Blasphemy / Relentless / Frailty** in that order. A simulator start on wave 1 keeps that fixed order and draws no random number; with a configured set, each is offered at the tier above the owned one, one the set has maxed is left out, and when all three are maxed there is no choice and the wave starts 5 ticks later as if one had been accepted. | Empty start: W-Strat and both captured wave-0 offers (B:1854, C:1444); a configured or maxed set: Policy |
| Later offers | Three distinct eligible next tiers, drawn without replacement in a stable modifier-id order from a wave-specific seeded stream. Upgrades of an owned modifier weigh **2**, new modifiers **1**. | W-Strat ("a preference towards upgrades"); the 2:1 weights are Policy, not a recovered server probability |
| Eligibility | Exclude tier-III modifiers, owned single-tier modifiers and effects the simulator does not support. Dynamic Duo and Red Flag are eligible only going into waves 7–11; Mantimayhem and Reentry are never offered going into wave 12. Volatility III is not newly offered in waves until its temporary pool works. Supported in waves: Bees!, Blasphemy, Doom, Dynamic Duo, Frailty, Myopia, Quartet, Red Flag, Relentless, Solarflare, Totemic and Volatility I–II; Mantimayhem and Reentry are deferred and never offered. Offers are shown in draw order (the game's are unsorted, e.g. C:1482). | Captures (no maxed or owned single re-offered, 30/30); W-Strat (before wave 7), W-Mod and W-News (after wave 11); the rest Policy |
| Ownership | The engine owns the outstanding offer; highlighting a choice is UI state. **Continue** sends one recorded `confirm-modifier` command carrying the wave and the modifier id. Reopening the choice never draws again. At execution the engine rejects a stale, duplicate, wrong-stage or ineligible choice. The next wave spawns through the scheduled wave lifecycle, not the practice-tagged spawn command. | Captures (identical arguments on reopen); the command contract is Policy |
| Specific pick | A simulator practice feature: besides the three on offer, the choice may be any modifier **eligible** going into that wave by the rules above (tier above the owned one, not maxed, the wave gates, Volatility III withheld, supported only), wave 1 included, where the gates are those of any wave. The snapshot's open offer lists them (`offer.eligible`, cache key order) beside the game's `options`. It is the same recorded `confirm-modifier` command, and it draws no random number: the offer is drawn as usual, so later offers and layouts are those any other pick would meet. A set that leaves wave 1 nothing on its fixed offer still opens no choice. | Policy (practice; the game only lets you take one of the three) |
| Live rules | A pick takes effect at the next T0, five ticks after the confirm, when the live modifier rules are refreshed; Frailty's new maximum (base − floor(base × 10/20/40%), never compounded) shows then. Current HP is only lowered to the new maximum, never healed; what the game does to HP already below it stays open. | Captures (99 → 90 → 80 at B:2975, C:3064, K:3992); current HP: C14 |
| Starts on waves 1–11 | Open directly on the start wave's choice, without the entry walk or talking to Minimus, with the player on (23,38) on the west side, facing east, and the camera looking east over the arena, somewhat zoomed out. The game's entry walk ends on (32,32) instead (B:1821–1827). A tick-zero change of start wave between the waves and Sol moves the player to that start's own tile and facing, as a fresh start would; a start tile the run was given, such as a replay's recorded one, is kept. The player may choose another floor tile (`colosseumStartTile`, `"x,y"`; one off the floor falls back to (23,38)); like the Inferno's practice start tile, a change applies from the next fresh start, and a tick-zero edit moves no one. A direct Sol start keeps its captured tile and camera and ignores the choice. | Policy (intentional shortcut; the tile, facing and view are the maintainer's choice) |
| Fight-start countdown | A start on waves 1–11 (an entry, Restart fight, Ctrl+R or the layout tool's Start) has no 3-2-1 countdown: it opens on the choice, and the player's Continue starts the wave five ticks later, so a countdown would only delay the choice. The run ticks from tick 0 as it does with the countdown off. A direct Sol start counts down as before; a replay takeover counts down whatever the start (`fightStartCountdown` in `modules.ts`). | Policy (maintainer's choice) |

## Layout

| Topic | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Warband | The wave owner draws the berserker's tile **uniformly** over x 29..35, y 34..40, places the trio at T0 by `WARBAND_SLOT_OFFSETS`, and adds the southern Quartet member when Quartet is owned. Warband behavior is the existing `warband.ts`. | Captures (box, formation 28/28); uniform: Policy |
| Spawn points | Initial non-warband NPCs take distinct points, drawn uniformly by the wave-layout stream from the twelve default spawns **clear of the player**: a point is left out when its SW tile is within Chebyshev 4 of the player's tile at the start of T0 (before T0's move, so waiting and then clicking on T0 is what counts). From the Wiki's start tile (23,36) that leaves out exactly the three west points; from the simulator's wave start (23,38) it leaves out (25,34) and (19,37). At most four points are ever within 4 of a floor tile, so a wave always has enough. | W-Strat (avoidance, the 5th-tick click, "the west area"); captures (nine points, no reuse, radius under 6, decision after T0−3); radius 4 (5 fits too), the start-of-T0 tile (T0−2 and T0−1 fit too) and uniform: Policy (A1) |
| Practice layout | A simulator practice feature for the **start wave only** (`colosseumSpawnLayout`, `"<wave>:<one char per point>"`; one for another wave, or anything invalid, is random). It puts some or all of the start wave's initial NPCs on chosen spawn points; the warband, the reinforcements, the manticores' first style, Dynamic Duo's partners and every later wave stay as they would be. The points are drawn as usual: chosen NPCs take their points, even one within 4 of the player, and the rest take the drawn points left free, in draw order, so the stream and every later draw are unchanged. The setup warns about what the game could not produce with the player waiting on the start tile: a chosen point within 4 of it (`spawn-too-close`), and the start tile under the NPC's footprint (`start-in-footprint`); the run honors them anyway. Recorded with the run's setup, so a replay rebuilds it. | Policy (practice, as the Inferno's spawn layouts) |
| West spawn points | (19,32), (25,34) and (19,37) come from third-party encounter recordings (not redistributed), by maintainer decision. | A2 |
| Reinforcement tiles | The column is drawn at T0: distinct x from {31, 32, 33}, uniformly from the wave-layout stream. The gate is chosen at T0+66 from the player's tile at the start of that tick: the one whose middle-column spawn tile, (32,48) north or (32,19) south, is nearer (Chebyshev, SW tile to the player's tile), **north on a tie**. North puts every footprint's SW tile on y = 48; south mirrors each footprint across the arena's middle (y → 69 − y), so its SW tile is y = 22 − size (19, 20, 21 for the minotaur, jaguar and shaman). | W-Main (nearer gate); captures (north row and columns; the measure fits all 25 reinforced waves, including C:1600, which the gate openings would send south); uniform, measure, tie and south tiles: Policy (A4, C3) |

## NPC behavior

Every NPC below takes stats from `monsters.json`, its max hit from its Wiki infobox, and is removed
at its captured death-sequence offset (+3 shaman, jaguar, javelin; +4 manticore, shockwave,
minotaur).

| NPC | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Serpent shaman | Standard greedy movement and the production line-of-sight check: one step per tick, including the add+1 walk-in, which stops only on melee contact (C19). Casts when, from its post-move tile, the player's start-of-tick tile is at range 1–10 with line of sight traced player → NPC. First cast no earlier than add+3, then a 5-tick cooldown. Protection is checked at the cast; a released cast lands even if the shaman dies. The projectile ends at max(80, 67 + 4d) cycles, but the hit lands at the observed cast+3 (d ≤ 8) or cast+4 (d 9–10), not by the colossi's flight formula. Max hit 28. | Captures (241/241 casts, A:3719, A:1967, C:1474); infobox; walk-in with the player adjacent: MODEL |
| Jaguar warrior | Size 2, greedy movement, cardinal melee after moving, 5-tick cycle, no attack before add+3 (the shared lockout; no jaguar reached the player earlier than add+5). One claw animation; three independent accuracy and damage rolls, all three splats on the attack tick. No leap, bleed, staggered splats or special pathing. | Captures (65/65 same-tick splats, C:1732); W-NPC (independent rolls); the lockout: Policy (A7) |
| Minotaur | 225 HP; passive +5 HP every 20 ticks from the spawn tick, at the end of its turn on spawn+20k, capped, no splat; the engine's generic regeneration stays off for it. First action add+3. 12812 paths greedily; cardinal melee lands +1; 5-tick cycle. Seq 10844 is an ally heal, never an attack on the player. | Captures (11/11 lives, 340/340 headbars; 66/66 melee; C:2478) |
| Minotaur heal selection | When ready and the player is not cardinally adjacent: heal the first non-minotaur, in NPC processing order, below 75% HP whose center is at most 7 tiles (Chebyshev) from the minotaur's center with center-to-center line of sight, to full. The quote's 10-tile scan is implied by the 7 and not checked separately. A heal costs 10 ticks before the next action. A diagonal player does not suppress the heal. | W-NPC Minotaur (Mod Arcane quote), which explains every captured witness; first-in-order tie, the 10-tick cost (one witness, C:2487–2488) and the diagonal case are Policy (A5, A6, A15) |
| Fremennik warband | No new pursuit or attack behavior. Its breadth-first routing is not reused for other species, whose pillar stalls matter. | Captures (28/28 waves) |
| Shared by javelin, shockwave, manticore | 3×3; attack when ready at box distance 1–15 with line of sight traced player → nearest NPC tile after movement; pillars block. Protection is checked at launch. No melee. One approach step on add+1, then the standard combat step, blocked by the other wave NPCs' footprints (not the warband, orbs, bees or totems). | Captures (698 attacks and throws; 7,572/7,581 colossus movement ticks) |
| Javelin Colossus | 5-tick cycle; ranged hit at cast+3, landing even if the colossus has died. A strict four-ranged, one-artillery cycle, carried across line-of-sight gaps. | Shockwave Colossus | Single-target magic every 5 ticks; projectile end 76 + 8d cycles; hit at floor(end/30), landing even if the colossus has died. | Captures (63/63; B:3293, C:3480) |
| Manticore | Charges 4 ticks after first gaining line of sight; the orbs appear over the next 3 ticks; throws at charge+6 or later once it has line of sight; three orbs on consecutive ticks, each landing +1; recharges 4 ticks after the throw (a 10-tick cycle with line of sight); holds charged orbs without line of sight. Its death cancels every orb not yet landed. The third orb is always melee (stab); the first two are ranged→magic or magic→ranged, **drawn 50/50 once per wave and shared by every manticore of the wave**. One that was ready when another threw is delayed until 5 ticks after that throw; one not yet ready is not. When both are ready on one tick the earlier in processing order throws first. | Captures (10/10 cancelled orbs, 6/6 pairs shared an order, 10/10 blocked ticks); W-NPC (copying, "ready"); stab: cache param 26; the per-wave draw and 50/50: Policy (10 magic-first vs 12 ranged-first; C7) |
