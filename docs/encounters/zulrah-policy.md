# Zulrah — simulator policy

Decisions the simulator takes where the [facts spec](./zulrah-facts-spec.md) runs out. Each is
registered, with what would settle it, in the [open questions](./zulrah-open-questions.md).

## Rules

- A wiki word without a number ("several seconds", "much more frequently") becomes one named
  constant, labelled POLICY beside the code.
- A cache id is used only for what its gameval name describes.
- Timing is composed from fixed per-turn and per-transition durations, never fitted to an overall
  fight length.

## Values

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Actor | One NPC for the whole fight. Between forms it is `hidden` and untargetable, then transforms at its next spot, keeping its HP |
| Player hits around the sink | POLICY, pending a capture of several halberd swings on S−1, each with a hit due on S. The player's hits due on S or later deal nothing and show no splat; one due on S−1 lands, and an attack started on S−1 is made but wasted (capture: hits due on S never splat there). The splat that followed on E+3 is not modeled (open questions, Timing). Recoil due from S is not dropped: it is held to E+3, the next form's first turn, with its damage (capture, 2 of 2) |
| Recoil | A Ring of recoil or charged Ring of suffering reflects floor(d/10)+1 of a damaging hit onto the NPC that dealt it, a tail slam included, on the next tick (capture), at most the NPC's remaining HP, and nothing onto an NPC dead by then. Shared combat code (`core/combat/recoil.ts`, `CombatSystem.queueRecoil`), so every encounter's NPC attacks recoil; environmental damage recoils only where it names its attacker, which here is the tail. A Ring of recoil's charges are not tracked, so it never breaks (simulator policy) |
| Opening | Rises on the fight's first tick (reported from play), at the middle spot facing south; attackable 4 ticks later and first barrage 11 ticks after the rise (capture) |
| Line of sight | A due ranged or magic attack (jad included) waits while Zulrah cannot see the player, retrying every tick, and goes off on the first tick it can; its 3-tick clock restarts there (capture). The check is the engine's NPC reach (`canNpcAttack`), a ray from the player's start-of-tick tile to the nearest tile of Zulrah's footprint. Barrages and snakeling orbs need no sight; the tail keeps its safe tiles |
| Line-of-sight give-up | Approved by the project owner, unverified. If the due attack is blocked on 8 consecutive ticks (`ZULRAH_LOS_GIVE_UP_TICKS`), t to t+7, Zulrah drops the phase's remaining attacks on t+7, and its next turn, the phase's first `after` special, or else its dive, comes on t+8. It reproduces Z2's green east phase (blocked from t3137, dive t3145). The six captured waits, blocked tick to next turn, were 5, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 10 ticks, probably random (facts spec, Line of sight) |
| Turns | Every attack, tail stare, cloud barrage and snakeling orb takes 3 ticks; each phase has its listed turns. A phase dives once its last turn's projectile has landed and its 3-tick turn is over (capture) |
| Form change | Sink to emerge 3 ticks, first turn 3 ticks after the emerge (capture). Drawn through SINKFAST's 60 cycles, then hidden for the tick before the emerge; attacks refused from the sink until 4 ticks after the emerge (capture: Z2 once, Z4 at four form changes), so its first turn comes a tick before the player can attack it. It stays targetable, with its health bar, until the sink tick |
| Facing | Faces (28,62) on surfacing, which from the middle spot is south, the opening rise's spawn angle (capture); the player on an attack turn; the locked tile during a tail stare; the first cloud's center while spitting venom, and the orb's tile while spitting a snakeling orb |
| Tail | Slam 6 ticks after a TAIL_LEFT stare starts and 5 after a TAIL_RIGHT; next stare 2 ticks after a slam; dive 3 ticks after the last slam (capture); 3x3; the stun blocks moving and attacking from the hit tick through 6 ticks after it (capture, without a knockback), spotanim 245 on the player; TAIL_LEFT if the player's start-of-tick tile is west of Zulrah's center column, TAIL_RIGHT if east (INFERENCE, 14 of 18 captured stares), TAIL_LEFT in the column itself (POLICY); no hit or stun on the three tiles behind each pillar (24,63 24,62 25,62 and 32,63 32,62 31,62) while at the middle spot (captured for 32,62; reported from play for the rest) |
| Clouds | 2 orbs per barrage landing 3 and 4 ticks later, each cloud 30 ticks (capture); 1–5 typeless a tick |
| Cloud sites (FOR DISCUSSION) | 9 sites around the island's U (capture). A captured phase repeats its captured sequence barrage by barrage; the opening and each rotation's last phase use the 4-barrage fill; every other barrage picks one of the 15 pairs of sites at most two steps apart on the U at random, each equally likely, either orb first, from the encounter's seeded cloud stream; every captured pair was within two steps. The counts are in the [facts spec](./zulrah-facts-spec.md#measured-in-the-captures) (Cloud sites). The player's position plays no part. The fill never uses (32,69), so the arm tips (24,70) and (32,70) stay clear; ordinary barrages have no tip rule. Which phases are captured, inferred or fallback: open questions, Clouds |
| Snakelings | Orb lands 4 ticks later (capture); 50/50 style; first attack 3 ticks after landing; magic range 7; 25% venom per attack; one left alone dies off 51 ticks after it appears, the most common of the captured 50–53, by the engine's scripted death, as a kill without a hit (capture) |
| Snakeling sites (FOR DISCUSSION) | Every orb lands on one of the 9 cloud sites' centers (capture), with no walkability check, since captured orbs landed on (33,70), off the walkable island ([facts spec](./zulrah-facts-spec.md#measured-in-the-captures), Snakeling orb). A captured phase repeats its captured orb sequence; every other orb picks a site the phase has not used yet at random, from the encounter's seeded snakeling stream. The player's position plays no part. Which phases are captured: open questions, Snakelings |
| Dive-tick landings | A cloud or snakeling whose orb lands on the dive tick appears on the next form's first turn (capture) |
| Attacks toggle | With Zulrah Attacks off the fight keeps its timing exactly: every turn is taken, an attack turn still waits for sight and gives up as above, and its style and rolls are still drawn from the encounter's seeded stream, as are a snakeling's, so turning attacks back on mid-fight continues with the rolls a run with attacks on throughout would make. The rotation, cloud-site and snakeling-site draws have streams of their own either way. Only the attacks, their damage, venom and visuals, and the tail's slam damage, stun and animation, are dropped |
| Blue | Magic 75% of the time |
| Rotations | Equally likely |
| Projectiles | 1044 ranged, 1045 cloud orbs, 1046 magic, 1047 snakeling orb; attacks fly 40→90 cycles at any distance (capture) |
| Venom immunity | The game's single poison counter (`core/poison.ts`): Antidote++ -40, anti-venom -41, anti-venom+ -50, extended anti-venom+ -59 steps, venom blocked below -38. The 30-tick step clock keeps its phase through drinks and envenoming and starts fresh only when the counter leaves 0 (capture), so a venom immunity drunk from clean is the top of its wiki range and one drunk over a running counter falls inside it. A re-sip resets to the potion's full value (reported by the project owner from play); a weaker potion over a longer running immunity keeps the longer one (owner-approved policy) |
| Arena | Map squares 35_47 and 35_48: the engine accepts any tile inside the arena bounds (`isInTileSpace`), and terrain heights are composed across both squares |
| Arena before the cache bake | The island's own 69 walkable tiles (`zulrahIslandTiles`, the pinned cache's bake tile for tile), without collision (no pillar line of sight) until the bake replaces it, as Sol Heredit serves its own arena; never the global default, which the cache sets to Yama's arena |
| Loadouts | The wiki's five inventory setups slot for slot, plus Ranged Only with a Twisted bow; substitutions are in the open questions |
