# Awakened Duke Sucellus — unverified behavior register

This register records the parts of the simulator model that the available evidence leaves open.
The [facts spec](./duke-facts-spec.md) holds the supported rules and defines the
[capture aliases](./duke-facts-spec.md#captures). The [evidence audit](./duke-capture-analysis.md#3-evidence-audit)
records the observations and the limits of each sample.

**MODEL** identifies a fit or extrapolation from the available evidence.
**POLICY** identifies a simulator choice. **UNVERIFIED** identifies a boundary the evidence leaves open.
A finite sample can rule out a proposed range or trigger and constrain the possibilities.
Exact roll distributions and hidden probabilities require a source for the distribution itself.

**E** = [`DukePracticeEncounter.ts`](../../src/encounters/dukePractice/DukePracticeEncounter.ts),
**A** = [`arena.ts`](../../src/encounters/dukePractice/arena.ts), and **P** =
[`controls.ts`](../../src/encounters/dukePractice/controls.ts).
